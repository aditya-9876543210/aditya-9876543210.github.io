01 / ASDRP · Numerical methods

AEGIS

Numerical methods for accurate smooth flow and stable shock capture, from Euler to ideal MHD.

Behind the research

I develop numerical methods that balance accuracy in smooth flow with stability near shocks. My AEGIS (Adaptive Edge-Guided Interface Scheme) research includes applications to the Euler equations and extensions to ideal magnetohydrodynamics.

Key finding

AEGIS outperformed traditional methods like WENO5-JS, more accurately simulating fluid flows with shocks and standard smooth flow.

02 / ASDRP · Plasma simulation

ASCFD

Computational models of plasma behavior and electric-propulsion systems.

Behind the research

I've built computational models to investigate electric-propulsion systems like Hall thrusters. The code (named ASCFD) was designed to be a 2D-Hyrbid Nonquasineutral code in order to achieve high physical accuracy when simulating plasma sheath effects within the thruster, such as wall erosion.

Key finding

ASCFD showed qualitative agreement with data from real Hall thruster operations, capturing a more complete physical profile of plasma-wall interactions compared to other models.
